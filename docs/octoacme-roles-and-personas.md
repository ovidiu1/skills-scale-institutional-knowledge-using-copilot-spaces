# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements and ensure the team understands the problem to solve.

### Responsibilities
- Gather requirements from stakeholders and business partners
- Clarify acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Maintain a shared understanding of scope and business rules
- Help validate delivered functionality against business outcomes

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to turn vision into actionable scope
- Partners with Developers to refine requirements and acceptance criteria
- Coordinates with Project Managers on priority, timelines, and dependencies

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements
- Improve alignment between business intent and implementation
- Support faster, more predictable delivery

### Typical Communication
- Requirement refinement sessions
- Acceptance criteria reviews
- Stakeholder requirement alignment meetings

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads ensure that proposed solutions are intuitive, usable, and aligned with customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Lead user research, design exploration, and usability validation
- Define interaction patterns, wireframes, and design guidance
- Partner with Product Managers on user-centered trade-offs
- Validate that delivered solutions meet usability expectations

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers on product direction and user outcomes
- Works with Developers to bridge design and implementation details
- Shares insights with Project Managers to help prioritize design work and manage dependencies

### Goals
- Deliver user-friendly solutions that reduce friction
- Ensure design quality and consistency across features
- Increase customer adoption and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- Usability test reports and design handoffs
- Cross-functional alignment meetings

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads focus on quality assurance across the delivery lifecycle and help minimize defects in production.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, acceptance and regression criteria
- Coordinate cross-team testing efforts and sign-off practices
- Track defects, test coverage, and release readiness
- Advise on automation, performance, and reliability requirements

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to shape testable requirements and resolve issues
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and definition of done
- Keeps Project Managers informed on quality risks and release readiness

### Goals
- Improve confidence in delivery quality
- Reduce rework and stabilize release execution
- Accelerate feedback loops through early testing

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions
- Defect and release readiness reports
- Quality checkpoints in sprint reviews

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, change control, and validation across release boundaries.

### Responsibilities
- Define release criteria and deployment plans
- Manage release windows, rollback plans, and environment readiness
- Communicate release status and risks to stakeholders
- Ensure verification and post-release monitoring are completed

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing with project plans
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on release readiness and deployment risks
- Keeps Stakeholders and Executive Sponsors informed of release status and impacts

### Goals
- Deliver releases smoothly and consistently
- Minimize deployment risk and business disruption
- Ensure clear accountability for release activities

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness meetings
- Deployment status updates
- Post-release validation and incident review notes

---

## Stakeholders / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Executive Sponsors champion the project, provide strategic direction, and unblock decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Sponsor the project and align it with organizational priorities
- Approve scope, funding, and key milestones
- Provide timely feedback on trade-offs and business impacts
- Support escalation and governance as needed

### Interaction with existing roles
- Engages with Product Managers and Project Managers for alignment and decision-making
- Consults with Business Analysts and UX Leads on strategic expectations
- Helps remove blockers and ensure the team has access to needed resources

### Goals
- Keep the project aligned with business goals
- Reduce delays caused by decision gaps or unmet dependencies
- Increase transparency for executive-level outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates
- Decision reviews and executive summaries
- Stakeholder alignment sessions

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps / Platform Engineers support infrastructure, deployment automation, and operational reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain deployment pipelines, observability, and infrastructure tooling
- Support production readiness, scalability, and incident response
- Collaborate on environment management and operational requirements
- Help define infrastructure-related technical risks and dependencies

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers on deployment and environment expectations
- Coordinates with Release Managers on readiness and release automation
- Advises Project Managers on infrastructure capacity and operational risk

### Goals
- Improve stability and repeatability of deployments
- Reduce infrastructure-related delays and incidents
- Enable faster, safer delivery through automation

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure planning sessions
- Deployment and monitoring handoffs
- Operational review syncs
